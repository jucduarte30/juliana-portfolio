Stop 01 — UX research & redesign

Navegante

Academic project, UX discipline · Individual work
Field research Figma Prototyping Public transit
Field research→ Pain points→ Feature redesign→ Figma prototype

Navegante is the public transit app used daily by thousands of people across Lisbon. Rather than starting from assumptions, I began with field research to understand what actually frustrated real riders — confusing navigation, unclear real-time information, friction in everyday tasks like planning a route.

Those findings shaped a set of redesigned features and flows, which I then built out as a fully interactive Figma prototype — covering the core navigation, route planning, and information architecture end to end.

Outcome

The redesign never shipped — it was a research project, not a production release. But going through the complete UX cycle, from field interviews to a working prototype, is what it actually taught me: how much a small, ordinary interaction shapes whether a system feels usable.

Stop 02 — Mobile development

A wizarding world, mapped onto yours

Mobile Computing coursework · Group project (design + Android development)
Kotlin Android Geolocation Sensors Gamification
Onboarding & identity→ Map exploration→ Spellcasting via motion→ Social layer

A Harry Potter–inspired Android app built to explore what a phone's own sensors can do for an experience, not just what a screen can show. Onboarding sorts each user into a house through a short quiz — like the Sorting Hat — and the interface's colours and identity shift to match.

The core loop uses the device's GPS and location services to place collectible items on a live map, encouraging players to physically move to reach them. Certain spells are cast by shaking or gesturing with the phone, read through the accelerometer — turning a real-world motion into an in-game action. A "Sorcerer's Room" tracks progress and stats, and a social layer lets players add friends, message, and trade items.

Outcome

Fully functional, not just a prototype — from onboarding through to a working spell and exploration system. I contributed to both the interaction design (house theming, onboarding flow, the map and inventory UI) and the Kotlin implementation.
